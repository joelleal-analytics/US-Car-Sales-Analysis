# Evaluating US Car Sales Trends, Regional Demand, and Market Share (2018-2024)
An Excel dynamic dashboard analyzing 1M+ rows of raw US car sales data from 2018 to 2024.

## Executive Summary
This project analyzes an expansive dataset comprising over **1,000,000 rows of real-world autimotive transaction records** spanning from 2018 to 2024 to evaluate market trends, regional demand, and multi-year profitability acorss major US automotive brands.

## Key Insights:
* **Macro Sales and Revenue Lifecycle:** Revenue plateaued and retorgraded between 2018 and late 2020, but followed a near-expoential hockey-stick growth trajectory starting 2021 that continued through 2024.
* **Geogaphical Revenue:** South and West macro-regions dominated the enterprise scale, outpacing the total combined volume of the Midwest and Northeast territories.
* **Demographic Profiling:** Target market shows an almost exact 50% Male to 50% Female gender equilirium. While age distribution modeling revealed that the majority of buying transactions were commanded by Middle age (40-59) and Seniors (60-70) brackets. 

## Tools and Concepts Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Large dataset standardization, data modeling, dynamic slicers, pivot tables, trend analysis
*  **Data Visualization:** KPI cards, customer demograpic segmentation, transaction capital methods, annual and regional bar charts.
