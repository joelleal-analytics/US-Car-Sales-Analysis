# US Car Sales Trends, Regional Demand, and Market Share (2018-2024)
An Excel dynamic dashboard analyzing 1M+ rows of raw US car sales data from 2018 to 2024.
## Executive Summary
This project analyzes over **1,000,000 transaction records** to evaluate market trends, regional demand, and multi-year profitability acorss major US automotive brands.

<img width="757" height="287" alt="Macro Sales and Revenue Lifecycle" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e03edc53-0d23-4233-ab8d-bf89275f5fb9" />

## Key Insights:
* **Revenue and Profit:** Evaluated over $86.7B in total revenue and $13.9B in profit across 4 major US regions.
* **Financing Breakdown:** Modeled customer payment distribution across cash (33%), loan (33%), and lease (34%).
* **Demographics:** Executive KPI cards, regional bar charts, and age demographic segmentation.
